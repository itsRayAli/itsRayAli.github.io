Research tool / Market data Private project

Edgewise.

Where the markets disagree.

The same question can carry different odds on different prediction markets. Edgewise puts those prices side by side, highlights meaningful gaps, and explains every signal it raises. It only runs when you ask, keeps its data on your own machine, and never places a trade. It’s a research tool, not a trading bot.

  • Market research
  • Data analysis
  • Local-first
  • Local AI

Odds, side by side

One row per prediction, with each exchange’s price next to the others.

Signals that explain themselves

Every flag shows its prices, volume, and exactly why it qualified.

Only when you ask

No background scanning. Every query is bounded, cancellable, and timestamped.

Test it on paper

A virtual portfolio follows signals without real money or real orders.
