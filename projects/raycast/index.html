RayCast.

Your media, organised. On any screen.

RayCast started as a way to get media onto the big screen and grew into a full media hub. It organises your local library into shows and movies, builds subtitles for your videos, and casts to a Chromecast, a browser tab, or your whole screen. It can also turn any other browser into a cast screen, and a phone companion app puts the controls in your hand. It all runs on your computer, so your media stays local.

  • Media library
  • Chromecast
  • Subtitles
  • Companion app
  • Local-first
