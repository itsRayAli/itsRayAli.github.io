Browser extension / AI conversations Personal project

ChatMap.

Find the conversation. Keep the idea.

ChatMap helps you find conversations across ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and Google Gemini. Search captured titles, prompts, and replies, bookmark useful chats, and jump back to the source. The library stays in your browser, with no ChatMap account, analytics, or external requests.

  • ChatGPT
  • Copilot
  • Gemini
  • Search & bookmarks
  • Local-first
View source ↗

One place to look

Search captured titles, user prompts, and assistant replies across three AI services, with phrase search and relevance sorting.

Keep useful conversations close

Bookmark chats, filter by platform or last-change date, and reopen the original conversation.

Bring older chats along

Import ChatGPT’s conversations.json export, or export and import a ChatMap JSON backup to move your library.

A library that stays local

Conversation data lives in IndexedDB in your browser profile. Chrome, Edge, and Firefox have separate builds and libraries.
