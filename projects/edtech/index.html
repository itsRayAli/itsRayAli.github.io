Educational technology University of Houston

Educational Technology.

Tools for teaching, learning, and events.

A collection of tools built for the University of Houston to save faculty and staff time, from making course materials accessible to grading at scale and running a STEM competition. Each one runs on AWS.

01

Accessibility · AI

PDF Accessibility

Accessible documents, faster.

Turns course PDFs into WCAG 2.1 accessible documents. AI suggests alt text and document details, scanned pages are read with OCR, and a visual editor lets you check tags and reading order before downloading. Works on one file or a whole batch.

Built with

  • ReReact
  • TSTypeScript
  • PyPython
  • λAWS Lambda
  • SFStep Functions
  • BrAmazon Bedrock
  • TxAmazon Textract
  • TfTerraform
02

Assessment · AI

Rubric Grader

AI-assisted grading at scale.

Helps instructors grade consistently. Upload a blank assignment and the AI builds a rubric from it, then run a batch of student submissions, including handwritten work, against that rubric. Each is scored for how closely it matches, with results ready to export or send as student feedback.

Built with

  • ReReact
  • TSTypeScript
  • PyPython
  • λAWS Lambda
  • SFStep Functions
  • BrAmazon Bedrock
  • DyDynamoDB
  • EnMicrosoft Entra ID
  • TfTerraform
03

Events · Registration

Mars Rover Celebration

A STEM event, run end to end.

Registration and event management for the Mars Rover Celebration, a University of Houston STEM Center competition for elementary and middle school students. Teachers, students, and judges register online. On the day, a full admin panel handles check-in, judge assignments, and scoring, with live updates. In English and Spanish.

Built with

  • NNext.js
  • TSTypeScript
  • PrPrisma
  • PgPostgreSQL
  • AmAWS Amplify
  • SEAmazon SES
  • CDAWS CDK
