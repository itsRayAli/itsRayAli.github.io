How I work

A few things I believe.

  1. 01

    Local-first where it matters

    Data stays on your own machine unless there’s a good reason to move it.

  2. 02

    Automate the repetitive, keep people in control

    Automation should remove busywork, not judgement. The final call stays with a person.

  3. 03

    Built to be run, not just demoed

    Tests, CI, installers, and containers from the start, so a project keeps working after launch day.

  4. 04

    Clear about the limits

    Every signal explains itself and every experimental feature is labelled. Knowing what something can’t do builds trust.
